\# Saarthi



\*\*An AI-assisted navigation module for a white mobility cane.\*\*



Saarthi is a proposed detachable navigation-assistance module designed to complement an existing white cane. The white cane remains the user's primary mobility aid.



\## Problem



People with visual impairments may benefit from additional information about nearby obstacles and their surroundings. Saarthi explores a low-cost, detachable electronic module that could provide supplementary tactile alerts and smartphone-based assistance.



\## Proposed System



The planned system consists of:



\- \*\*ESP32 development board:\*\* Intended to process sensor readings and support Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) communication.

\- \*\*Distance sensor:\*\* Intended to detect nearby obstacles in front of the user.

\- \*\*Vibration motor and driver:\*\* Intended to provide tactile alerts.

\- \*\*Android phone:\*\* Planned to support camera-based AI features, scene descriptions and text-to-speech.

\- \*\*Detachable mount:\*\* Intended to attach the module to an existing white cane.



\## Planned Development



1\. Finalize component selection and system architecture.

2\. Develop and test basic distance-sensor readings.

3\. Implement vibration alerts through a suitable motor driver.

4\. Explore BLE communication between the ESP32 and an Android phone.

5\. Develop smartphone-based assistance features.

6\. Integrate the components and evaluate the prototype safely.



\## Current Status



\*\*Stage: Planning and initial repository setup.\*\*



The hardware and software have not yet been implemented or validated. Features described above are proposed development goals, not completed capabilities.



\## Safety



Saarthi is intended as a supplementary aid, not a replacement for a white cane, orientation and mobility training, or other established mobility aids. Its effectiveness and safety must be evaluated before real-world use.
